Finiphi Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

Finiphi is a personal and small-business finance tracker. This policy explains what the app stores, how it handles files, and what choices you have.

Information you enter

Finiphi can store the optional display name and currency preference you enter, plus financial records such as transaction dates, descriptions, categories and amounts; budgets; savings goals and contribution sources; recurring bills; and preferences. If you import a bank CSV, the transaction fields you choose to import are stored as app records.

Where information is stored

Your app records are stored locally in Finiphi on your device. Finiphi does not create an online account, connect to your bank, sync your records to a Finiphi server, or transmit your financial records to the developer. The Android app does not include analytics or advertising software and does not request internet access.

Files you choose to import or export

When you choose a CSV or Finiphi share file, the app reads that file to perform the import or show the shared dashboard. When you export a PDF, CSV, backup or share file, Android asks you to choose a save location. The selected file is handled by the storage provider you choose; a provider may sync files according to its own settings.

CSV, PDF and backup files are not encrypted by Finiphi. Keep exported files private. Dashboard share files are encrypted on the device using the access code you set. Send the access code separately from the share file. Anyone who has both can view the shared information, and a recipient may copy or forward it.

Sharing and disclosure

Finiphi does not sell or share app records with the developer or advertisers. You control whether to export files or give an encrypted share file and its code to another person. Android and the file-storage provider you select may process files as part of providing their services and are governed by their own privacy terms.

Data deletion

Use Settings → Delete all app data to remove the records stored in Finiphi. Uninstalling the app also removes its app-local records. Files you previously exported or saved outside the app are not deleted by this action; remove those separately from the location where you saved them.

Children

Finiphi is a general finance tool and is not designed for children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app’s handling of information changes. The current version and effective date will be shown here. For privacy questions or requests, contact finiphi@officially.co.za.
